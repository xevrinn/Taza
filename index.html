Built for the people
responsible for water.

A focused operational product for salt-production facilities and monitoring teams — with a path from one pilot to broader environmental intelligence.

01

Observe

Connect future salinity, temperature and pH sensors to an audited site record. Establish a baseline and identify missing observations.

02

Understand

Connect location, historical context and configurable triggers in one workspace. Separate measured evidence from projections.

03

Respond

Generate site reports and route validated alerts to operators. A traceable decision is better than scattered spreadsheets.
